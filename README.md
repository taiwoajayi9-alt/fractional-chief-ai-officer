## The problem

Most leadership teams know AI matters. Few have someone senior who owns it. Teams buy tools on their own, pilots start and stall, nobody has said what data can go where, and the board keeps asking for the plan.

A full-time Chief AI Officer is expensive and hard to find. A fractional one gives you the same leadership for the days you need.

## What I do

1. **AI strategy and roadmap.** Pick the few AI opportunities tied to your goals, each with an owner, a budget and a target.
2. **Governance and policy.** Set the rules on approved tools, data and risk, so people can use AI with confidence.
3. **Tools and vendors.** Decide what to buy, what to build and what to ignore, and hold suppliers to what they promise.
4. **Delivery oversight.** Keep AI projects moving, unblock teams and measure what each one returns.
5. **Team capability.** Train leaders and teams to use AI in their own work, and help you hire the right people.
6. **Board reporting.** Give the board a clear view of progress, risk and return, in plain language.

## How it works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Two-week readiness sprint** | I interview your leaders, review how AI is used today and give you a prioritised roadmap. You can stop there. |
| **A monthly retainer** | A set number of days each month as your AI lead: in leadership meetings, owning the roadmap and unblocking teams. |
| **Handover** | When you're ready, I help you hire or grow a full-time AI lead and hand the work over. |

Not sure where to start? The [AI maturity assessment](https://taiwoajayi9-alt.github.io/fractional-chief-ai-officer/assessment/) takes about 7 minutes and gives you your stage, a score for each area and next steps.
