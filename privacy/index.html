This site is run by Taiwo (Tai) Ajayi, Lagos, Nigeria, who is responsible for the personal information described here. In this notice, "we" means Tai Ajayi.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and store responses. They process information only on our instructions:

  • Google, which stores enquiries and assessment responses in Google Sheets (through Google Apps Script) and delivers our notification emails through Gmail.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), which hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces on our pages. Your browser requests them directly from Google, which receives your IP address.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

International transfers

Our service providers may store information outside the country where you live, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you
  • correct it if it's wrong
  • delete it
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to it
  • give you a copy in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything we did before

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Nigeria that is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, and in Portugal the CNPD. We'd appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

How to contact us

Send a request through the enquiry form and write "Privacy request" in the message, or message us on LinkedIn. We'll respond within 30 days.
